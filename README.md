# ☁️ Cloud Thief

### Steal the fluff. Dodge the patrol. Escape the skies.

**Cloud Thief** is a kawaii fantasy platform adventure where you play as a tiny masked cloud thief equipped with a powerful cloud vacuum.

Leap across floating worlds, vacuum up magical clouds, avoid the Sky Patrol, and escape before you get caught.

---

## 🎮 The Game

Your mission is simple:

**Run → Jump → Vacuum → Escape**

Travel through whimsical floating worlds while collecting enough clouds to fill your vacuum tank.

But you're not alone.

The Sky Patrol is watching.

Drones patrol the skies, searchlights sweep across platforms, and getting spotted can quickly turn a peaceful cloud-stealing mission into a chase.

Once your cloud tank is full, find the extraction point and make your escape.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Controls |
|---|---|
| Move | `A / D` or `← / →` |
| Jump | `Space`, `W` or `↑` |
| Double Jump | Jump again while airborne |
| Vacuum | `Z` or `Shift` |
| Pause | `Esc` |

Touch controls are available on supported mobile devices.

---

## ☁️ How to Play

### 1. Explore

Run and jump across the floating environment.

### 2. Find Clouds

Look for collectible clouds hidden throughout each level.

### 3. Vacuum

Face the cloud and activate your vacuum.

Watch as the cloud stretches, swirls and gets sucked into your backpack.

### 4. Avoid the Sky Patrol

Patrol drones guard the skies.

Stay out of their path and watch out for searchlights.

### 5. Fill Your Cloud Tank

Collect enough clouds to complete the mission quota.

### 6. Escape

Once your tank is full, reach the extraction point.

Mission complete. ✨
