Drop the Cloud Thief opening animation in this folder as:

    opening.mp4     (H.264 - plays everywhere)   and/or
    opening.webm    (VP9/VP8 fallback)

It autoplays muted and loops on the first screen, is scaled to fit the whole window (never cropped),
and an invisible, keyboard-accessible Play button is placed over the painted "PLAY NOW" button.
If the painted button sits somewhere other than the default spot, edit OPENING.playRect in index.html
(fractions of the video frame: cx, cy, w, h). With no video here the game shows the illustrated title.
